Where the history effect is carried

Mech v2 results for Silent Influence. A judge told "previously scored 10/10" vs "2/10" shifts its score by about 2 points. The released single-token patch recovered about 0% of that. These tests find the site that carries it, freeze it on discovery tasks, and validate it once on 72 unseen tasks per model.

1. Released site vs the right site

Normalized move toward the source condition (1 = full effect transferred). Patching one final-input token does nothing in either model; patching all post-history positions after the read window transfers almost everything.

5. Free generation: knockout works, prompt-only patch does not

Greedy generation on 36 held-out tasks. Left: share of the greedy score gap removed by the frozen knockout. Right: shift of the greedy score toward the source after patching prompt states only, in score points (full gap ≈ 2).

Likely reason: every generated token is itself a post-history position and re-reads the history in the window; the knockout blocks that, the prompt patch does not. Confirming test (patch + generated-token knockout) not run.

Pre-registered success rules

Limits

  • No head-level circuit: attention-ranked top-10 head sets removed 4% / −6% of the gap, at random-set level; single heads have opposing signs.
  • Qwen pre-registered window prediction (blocks 9–17) was not met; reading spans 9–26. Qwen controls are 9 blocks wide vs 18 for the target.
  • Readout deviation: the pre-registered gate chose a "forced" readout whose positive control failed (58%); switched to the direct readout after S1, before any S2 data.
  • Explicit-mask vs default attention path differs by up to 0.18 (Llama) / 0.48 (Qwen) in E[score]; all knockouts are compared to mask-path baselines.
