Silent Influence · Context controls

We hold a question and its answer fixed, then change a sentence about the answer author’s past interaction. The original study found a 1.17-point score decrease across four models, with history unmentioned in 98.4% of concordantly labelled self-low explanations.

Original study: 234 tasks, 900 complete matched pairs; 1.17-point decrease, 95% CI [1.10, 1.24]. Context mention: 898/913 explanations. The follow-up experiments below test rubric sensitivity and the proposed internal intervention.
